Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

BlueShape15 is a personal app. It posts videos to the TikTok account you connect. It is not a public product and does not sell data.

What we store

  • TikTok client key, client secret, and a refresh token so the app can upload videos you generate.
  • Story text, video files, and posting progress needed to run the pipeline.

How it is used

Tokens and video files are used only to render and publish to your TikTok account. They are kept in your own environment (this computer and GitHub Actions secrets for this project).

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. TikTok receives the video and caption when you (or the scheduled publisher) post. See TikTok’s own privacy policy for how they handle that upload.
